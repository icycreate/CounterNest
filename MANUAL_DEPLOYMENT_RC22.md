## Retention and cleanup
Retained and failed-upload images are intentionally not deleted by the app. Use a privileged maintenance process only after confirming an object is not referenced by ANY `customer_display_configs.about_us.image_path`. Retain old objects for your rollback period and wait at least one hour beyond the last possible signed URL. Recheck references immediately before deletion; coordinate with editors to avoid deleting an upload between upload and publication. Never delete referenced objects. Include the new bucket in your storage backup/restore procedure; older backup helper lists may not include it.

## Rollback
Keep the additive schema and retained images when reverting frontend assets. Do not drop configuration columns/bucket as an emergency frontend rollback: doing so loses saved content. Restore a prior content snapshot through an authorized admin workflow if needed. Existing signed URLs may remain valid until expiry. No production data was touched in this task.
