# Cashier guide
Sign in with your own staff account. Use the eye control to show/hide a password when safe. If asked to change a temporary password, enter current, new and confirmation before continuing.

After login, Dashboard opens. An administrator opens Daily Cash before cash business begins; do not enter opening cash at login.

Open New Sale, select products and the intended variant/combo options, then confirm the quoted promotion, discount/reward, tax and total. Sold-out or disabled items cannot be assumed available from an old screen. Refresh and review any changed-price response.

Choose the payment method and enter cash tender accurately. Confirm the receipt and change. Cash tender, receipt, stock and cash ledger commit together in RC1.

If the result is uncertain, **do not collect payment again**. Use the recovery dialog to retry the same checkout intent; the server returns the original receipt if it already completed. Do not create a new cart to work around recovery. If you lose the tab/reference, ask an administrator to inspect Sales, payment records and stock before charging again.

Use Customers for history, loyalty and cards. Use Pre-Orders / Custom Cakes for pickup/deadline, deposits and balance payment; complete the linked order rather than selling the same goods independently. Send kitchen items through the preparation workflow.

Refunds require the appropriate manager authorization and a reason. Select the intended quantity/return-to-stock choice and verify cash and stock afterward. Report issues without passwords, secret keys or customer payment data.

For catalogue requests, review the request inbox and use only Contact via the preferred method. Prepare/copy/open does not send a message or mark the person contacted. Record the status after actual contact. Another method requires administrator authorization and a reason. Requests are not paid orders; never deduct stock or charge them independently of their linked scheduled order.
