# Inventory and receiving guide
Create/choose the supplier and purchase order. Receive the actual ingredient quantity, unit, cost, lot and expiry; verify the receipt before posting. A receipt must not be posted twice to recover a slow response—check receiving history first.

Inventory reflects sales, returns, production, conversions and adjustments. Edit product descriptions/prices through Products; editing a product no longer submits a stock balance. Use the stock-count workflow for physical corrections.

For Set Quantity, count goods and review the displayed current balance. RC1 locks/checks that balance on the server. If another sale or receipt changed it, the count is rejected and refreshed. Reconcile what happened, recount if necessary and save again. Do not blindly repeat the old count.

Record waste with a reason. Use lot controls for holds, expiry, FEFO and recall tracing; do not restore recalled/expired goods through a simple quantity correction. Receiving, conversion, production and variance should retain links and audit history. Reconcile stock movements and lot totals before accepting a discrepancy.
