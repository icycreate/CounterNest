# Production and kitchen guide
Preparation / Kitchen shows the order queue. Check order number, station, payment and notes. Move through Received → Preparing → Ready → Collected as work actually progresses. The customer ready display shows public order numbers; keep private customer details on staff screens.

Production Studio contains recipe guidance and training. Admins also use planning, batches, demand forecast and shortages under Production Planning. Approve the correct recipe, yield, ingredient substitutions and lot selections before completing work.

Record ingredient conversions with their input/output units and measured quantities. Conversion consumes input lots and creates traceable output lots. Complete a production batch once; record real yield, waste and variance through their own workflows. Do not repeat a completion after a network uncertainty until checking batch history.

Use usable lots in FEFO order. Quarantined, recalled or expired stock must not enter production. Trace exceptions and document authorized disposal/substitution. Update scheduled order deadlines and linked batch status without duplicating stock consumption. Reconcile finished goods against the kitchen order and today's planned pickup.
