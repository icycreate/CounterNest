# Phase 34.6 — Recipe Preparation & Step-by-Step Instructions

Recipes now support an introduction, preparation/cook time, temperature, equipment, allergen/cross-contact notes, storage/cooling notes, ordered instructions, staff checkpoints, optional private step photos, administrator approval, and a printable recipe card.

## Workflow
Recipe → Preparation Guide → Steps/photos/checkpoints → Save → Approve → Print/Use in production.
