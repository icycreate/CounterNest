# Phase 35.5 — Internal Production Acceptance & Training

Before daily use, complete the cumulative acceptance checklist covering conversions, recipe instructions/printing, scaling, costing, smart scheduling, variance, substitutions, customer status, supply-document review, desktop recovery/logging, and role-specific training.

Training roles:
- Administrator: setup/approval/cost/planning/variance/document review.
- Production staff: approved guide, checkpoints, actual usage, waste, substitution escalation.
- Cashier: customer request loading, fulfillment status, final checkout authority.
