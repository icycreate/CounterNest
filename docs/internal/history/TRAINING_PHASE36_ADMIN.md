# Phase 36 training — Admin

1. Configure catalogue descriptions, tags, allergens, dietary labels and notes. Validate labels with the business before publishing.
2. Create variants under the existing product; select shared or independent inventory at creation. Add unique SKUs/barcodes, prices, recipe scales and fallback costs. Never duplicate a product master to represent a variant.
3. Receive independent stock once with a traceable reason and best-before date. Approved independent production transfers its finished output automatically; do not receive those same units again.
4. Build combo definitions with fixed variants or category choices. Verify separate item value and sale allocations using a small test transaction.
5. Create preorder/cake quotes with a known customer and deadline. Approve the linked production plan; cancel old approved jobs before changing order quantities/deadline. Refund outstanding deposits before cancelling an unpaid order.
6. Review forecasts and explicitly approve product/variant quantities. Review shortage job lists, supplier/last price and usable lot balances; dismiss only with a reason or resolve after receiving stock.
7. Commission customer URLs/QR, TV timing and local station labels. Complete the UAT checklist and review commercial audit events before go-live.

Training evidence: record staff, date, device, observed outcomes and manager sign-off in the release UAT evidence.
