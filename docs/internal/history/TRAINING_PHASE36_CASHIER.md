# Phase 36 training — Cashier

1. Open the daily cash/drawer workflow as usual. Select products through the catalogue details so variants/options and combo choices are explicit. Variant barcodes select the configured variant.
2. Load customer orders into an empty cart at the correct station. Review refreshed prices; a server quote change blocks payment until the new total is confirmed. A selection does not reserve stock.
3. Finish ordinary sales with cash/card/QR. Receipts list selected variants and bundle components; full sale revenue and component costs feed existing reports.
4. For scheduled pickups, open Pre-Orders / Custom Cakes, search customer/order and record deposits already received. Cash deposits need an open drawer. Use Final POS payment for the remaining balance; do not charge the full amount again in an unrelated cart.
5. After payment, the Sales screen has the receipt showing full total, deposit and final balance paid. Record external card/QR payments/refunds through the normal business payment system.
6. Collection requires a paid ready order. Use the existing authorized sale refund process for a paid order; an old deposit cannot be refunded a second time.

Training evidence: record staff, date, device, observed outcomes and manager sign-off in the release UAT evidence.
