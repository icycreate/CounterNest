# Phase 36 training — Catalogue Display

1. Open the commissioned standalone catalogue URL. This screen has no staff/POS sidebar and no cart. Product cards open large details and thumbnail/swipe galleries.
2. Use Fullscreen after a user gesture. Turn TV auto-slide on/off; management controls idle return and slide duration. The QR opens the customer selection catalogue on a phone.
3. Test search/category/tag/price/availability/badge/allergen/dietary filters. Filters use management labels; unconfigured allergen data is shown as unknown and should be checked with staff.
4. Open the separate ready-board URL for preparing/ready numbers. Enable sound with a tap only after checking speakers and browser audio permissions. Public screens never show customer names, notes or payment amounts.
5. Leave network access available and prevent unwanted device sleep according to business policy. If a screen is stale after an upgrade, reload it or clear that site’s cached app data.

Training evidence: record staff, date, device, observed outcomes and manager sign-off in the release UAT evidence.
